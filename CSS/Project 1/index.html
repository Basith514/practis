Cyber Security

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting digital devices, networks, and sensitive data from unauthorized access, theft, and malicious attacks.As our world relies more on technology, protecting information becomes vital for both people and businesses. Cyber threats like phishing, malware, and ransomware aim to steal data, lock systems, or cause financial harm. An effective defense combines smart technology, clear rules, and aware people. Essential tools include firewalls, antivirus software, and encryption to block bad traffic and keep destructive cyber crimes.
